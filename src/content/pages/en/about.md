---
title: About Shifa-e-Rooh
---

*Shifa-e-Rooh* — "healing of the soul" — is a personal space for reflection on ethics, character and meaning.

The writings here are rooted in the Islamic philosophical and spiritual tradition, and they look closely at the values of the modern secular world: where they meet revelation, where they part from it, and what each can teach us about living well.

Articles are published in English, Urdu and Arabic.
